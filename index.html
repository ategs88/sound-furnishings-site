The sound of vintage

Our stereo consoles are decades old, and they sound like it. That's their charm. You'll hear a warm, room-filling sound, with a little crackle and some dust in the mix. It isn't a perfectly clear sound, and that's what makes it vintage.

Like any vintage audio, they like regular care. The controls and switches benefit from periodic cleaning, and the turntable's stylus and your records should be kept clean too. The stylus wears over time and will eventually need replacing. A little crackle when you turn a knob, along with the surface noise that comes with vinyl, is normal and to be expected. Ask us if you have questions.

Before you buy

Policies

All sales are final

We don't offer refunds, exchanges, or store credit, and every piece is sold as is. Most of what we sell is 50, 60, or 70 years old and has age-appropriate wear. Please zoom in on the photos before you buy. If you'd like more photos, a close-up of a particular spot, or have any question at all, email us at contact@sound-furnishings.com before purchasing.

Pickup

Pick up during store hours. If you're picking up yourself, bring enough help to load the piece into your vehicle, because our staff can't assist with loading. Bring your ID, blankets, plastic wrap, straps, and anything else you'll need to get it home safely. If someone else is picking up for you, let us know in advance so we know you've given permission.

Delivery

Some items may qualify for curbside delivery. You'll need to have help available to get the piece inside your home, and we aren't responsible for damage once the item has been delivered.

You can also hire a delivery company, and Atomic can suggest local ones. For out-of-state customers, we use a white glove shipping service and can get you a quote. Using a company we recommend doesn't waive storage fees or make us responsible for damage in transit.
