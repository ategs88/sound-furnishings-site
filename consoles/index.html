Every vintage stereo console we bring in is a one-of-a-kind piece of furniture with the warm sound only vintage speakers can make.

No two alike

Vintage stereo consoles were built to be the centerpiece of a room: a furniture cabinet with the music inside. No two of ours are the same. Each one has its own cabinet, its own character and its own sound, and what we have changes as new ones come in.

Every console is cared for by someone with years in audio engineering. Come hear one for yourself.

Questions

Where can I see your consoles?

In our space inside Atomic Furnishing & Design, 178 Broadway St, Asheville, NC 28801. Atomic is open every day.

Are all of your consoles listed online?

No. Some pieces are listed on Atomic's website and others are only on the floor. Send us a message or call Atomic at (828) 774-5024 to ask what is available.

Can I ask about a console before I visit?

Yes. Use the contact form or call Atomic. Tell us what you are looking for and we will let you know what we have.

Why do vintage speakers sound different?

Many listeners describe the sound of vintage console speakers as warm and full. The best way to understand it is to hear one in person.
