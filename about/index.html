About Sound Furnishings

Made to
live on.

We bring mid-century modern furniture and vintage stereo consoles into new homes.

What we do

Mid-century furniture was made decades ago, and the pieces we choose have stood the test of time. We find them, care for them and bring them to our space inside Atomic Furnishing & Design, so each one can live on in another home.

What we look for

We carry a lot of teak and walnut, along with other woods and materials from the mid-century era. What matters to us is quality, character and a piece that is ready to be used. Every piece has its own story, and we like to share it.

Our consoles

Stereo consoles are our specialty. Each one is a one-of-a-kind piece, cared for by someone with years in audio engineering, with the warm sound only vintage speakers can make.
